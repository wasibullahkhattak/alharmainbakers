Our Story

More Than Just a Bakery

Al-Harmain Bakers & Super Store was founded by Haji Inayat Ullah Khatak with a vision to bring the finest baked goods and everyday essentials to the people of Dera Ismail Khan. What started as a beloved bakery has grown into a full-service super store.

Nestled in the heart of Liaqat Town, Zafar Abad, we serve our community with handcrafted breads, heavenly cakes, and authentic pastries — alongside a complete range of groceries, beverages, dairy products, snacks, and household items. Your one-stop shop for everything!
